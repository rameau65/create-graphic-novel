# Vertical-scroll direction

## Scroll rhythm

Treat blank vertical space as time. Use:

- short gutters for rapid exchange or motion
- medium gutters for ordinary transitions
- long gutters for suspense, descent, contemplation, or reveal
- isolated full-width cuts for climax and emotional landing

Do not reproduce a print-page grid inside a long canvas. Compose in readable scroll units and test at phone width.

## Segment plan

For every cut record:

`cut | width/height role | visual | action | camera | continuity | top gutter | bottom gutter | text-safe area | transition`

Keep conversational pairs close enough to maintain connection. Delay a reveal below the fold with intentional empty space or a detail cut. Repeat establishing information when a long scroll gap risks spatial confusion.

## Production constraints

- Generate manageable cut groups, then assemble the long strip.
- Maintain one canonical canvas width and consistent side margins.
- Avoid placing text or critical detail across image-slice boundaries.
- Verify that exports meet the target platform's current size and file limits before finalizing.
- Preserve a master long strip and platform-specific sliced exports separately.
